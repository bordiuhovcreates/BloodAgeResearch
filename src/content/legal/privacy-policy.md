BloodAge Research («we», «us» or «our») respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit our website (the «Website»), request research access, propose a partnership or subscribe to our updates, how we use it and what rights you have. We process personal data in accordance with the Law of Ukraine «On Personal Data Protection» and, for visitors from the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 («GDPR»).

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is BloodAge Research, Kyiv, Ukraine. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at [hello@bloodage.com](mailto:hello@bloodage.com) or +38 (067) 245 18 64.

## 2. Definitions

- **Personal data** — any information relating to an identified or identifiable person, such as a name, email address or phone number.
- **Processing** — any operation performed on personal data, such as collecting, storing, using or deleting it.
- **Processor** — a third party that processes personal data on our behalf, for example a hosting or messaging provider.

## 3. Data we collect

We only collect the data needed to respond to you:

- **Research access and partnership forms:** your name, email address and phone number.
- **Updates subscription:** your email address.
- **Technical data:** when you open the Website, your browser automatically shares technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit with our hosting and font providers. We do not use this data to identify you.

The Website does not ask for and does not collect health data, blood test results or other medical information. Please do not send such information through our forms. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on the Website.

## 4. Why we use your data

- To answer your request for research access or a partnership — on the basis of steps taken at your request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- To send our research updates — on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can unsubscribe at any time.
- To keep the Website secure and working properly — on the basis of our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

## 5. Who receives your data

Your data is only shared with service providers that help us run the Website and handle requests:

- **Google** (Google Sheets, Google Apps Script and Google Fonts) — receives form submissions, stores them in a secure spreadsheet and delivers the fonts used on the Website.
- **GitHub** (GitHub Pages) — hosts the Website.

These providers act on our instructions. Some of them may process data outside Ukraine and the European Economic Area; in that case, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 6. How long we keep your data

- Research access and partnership requests are kept for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless a collaboration follows — then we keep the data for as long as the collaboration requires or the law obliges us to.
- Subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have your data deleted;
- restrict or object to processing;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

To exercise any of these rights, email [hello@bloodage.com](mailto:hello@bloodage.com). We respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or, if you are in the European Union, with your local data protection authority.

## 8. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access to the systems that store form submissions.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update shown at the top.
